02 / HOW I WORK

Built with AI.
Reviewed with intent.

AI is part of my process, from implementation to review. These projects document the requirements, engineering choices, and checks around the generated code.

  1. 01

    Define the boundary

    Make the allowed behavior explicit: who can access a resource, which state changes are valid, and what must be rejected.

  2. 02

    Examine the failure

    Look at interrupted requests, repeated actions, conflicting updates, and malformed inputs—not just the successful path.

  3. 03

    Keep the evidence

    Use regression cases and recorded checks to explain what was tested. Keep local verification and production readiness separate.
